A comprehensive guide to buying tuna

The Tuna Buyers Guide aims to support buyers in making informed, socially responsible, and environmentally sound sourcing decisions.

  • De-risk your supply chain

    Know where the biggest risks lie when you buy tuna.

  • Reduce human rights risks

    Protect the people who catch your tuna, and your brand.

  • Reduce environmental risk

    Back fisheries that support long-term ocean biodiversity.

  • Expert analysis

    Expert views from representatives offering perspectives from both policy and markets angles.

From the guide

10 guiding principles

The 10 Guiding Principles of the Tuna Buyers Guide provide a good framework for your business to integrate environmental stewardship with social responsibility.

  • Fair labour
  • Worker voice
  • Healthy ecosystems
  • Limit bycatch
  • Fisheries management
  • Community-centred
  • No harmful subsidies
  • Nature-positive
  • End ghost gear
  • Eliminate IUU fishing

Speakers

Everyone is welcome

The webinar is designed primarily for seafood buyers and procurement professionals, with broader relevance for retailers, sourcing directors, distributors, supply chain directors, importers, heads of quality assurance, and sustainability and ESG leads.

Whether you're an IPNLF member or simply interested in where your tuna comes from, you're invited to join us.
